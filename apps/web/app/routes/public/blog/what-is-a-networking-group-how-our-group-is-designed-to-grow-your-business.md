As a small business owner, you know first hand that running a business can be difficult. It can often seem like an uphill battle with minimal support. You don’t have the security of supervisors, company policies or guidelines.

This lack of security also means a lack of restrictions, and it can be exciting. Perhaps it’s one of the reasons you decided to start your own business in the first place.

However, you may find yourself starting to feel alone and desire a connection with other business owners who can relate to your struggles and offer advice and support. If this sounds like you, joining a networking group may be a good next step for you and your business.

### **What is a Networking Group?**

A networking group is a group of individuals looking to share and expand their knowledge in the business world. Sometimes these are groups of people in the same industry exchanging knowledge of trends and job opportunities, and other times they are from different industries altogether.

However, the main objective of a networking group is to exchange referrals. Referrals are when someone recommends your product or service to someone they know. Groups can create a valuable word of mouth referral process, where you have a group that sells for you and gives you referrals that are ready to purchase and move forward.

A networking group is an organized referral network to help expand and promote your business, while doing the same for the others in your group.

### **Who Are Networking Groups For?**

Networking at its core is about connections. Building relationships and establishing connections with like-minded people is an integral part of growing a business, so networking is essential for any business owner.

Networking groups are for business owners and entrepreneurs who are looking to grow their network, and find a community of business professionals that care about your success. Instead of only networking with a bunch of people that you only meet once, or going to a convention once a year, you develop and build close relationships with a group of people.

As a solo entrepreneur, this can be a good solution to feeling lonely or stuck in your business.

If you are a business owner and you want more camaraderie, referrals, and advice from other powerful businesses, and professionals that you respect, then networking groups are right for you.

### **What Kinds of Groups are There?**

When considering groups, there are several options to choose from. There are local face-to-face groups, local paid groups, virtual paid groups and virtual free groups.

Local free groups tend to be a bit more casual and laid back then paid groups. They also tend to be face-to-face. These are a good option if you’ve never attended a networking event or group before, and want to test the waters. They’re also good to meet the local business community, especially if you are relatively uninvolved in your community.

Paid networking groups can be an extremely valuable tool for your business, depending on the quality of the group itself. Since you are investing your time and money for paid groups, you want to make sure they prioritize referrals and support, and show a track record of a ROI. The best paid groups are the ones where everyone inside has gotten more business as a direct result of the group’s referrals.

It is also important to look for a group that aligns with your values, and is focused on providing a positive environment where you can grow and learn.

For a lot of groups, joining is simply a matter of showing up. However, every group is different. If they have a website, it should tell you any restrictions or rules for becoming a member.

If it’s a paid membership, you’ll have to submit a payment and application to become an official member.
