**From the Biz President’s Desk:**

Hi there! I’m Jennifer Gilmore Sawyer and I’m the new owner of Biz to Biz Global Networking dba Biz to
Biz Now and/or Biz to Biz.

Wow, typing that and re-reading it is a bit overwhelming to me because I have so many emotions
attached to the Biz to Biz organization!! I feel honored and blessed to have been given the opportunity
to make a positive impact on the micro-small business community and to lead such an incredible group
of people along their journeys to … their version of success in business, but also in their personal lives as
well. Since really, one affects the other, right?

I’ve been involved with Biz to Biz since 2007 as both an employee and also as a member. Over the
years, I’ve experienced and witnessed 100s of testimonials from Biz members regarding their successes,
growth and development (both personally and professionally) and I’ve also made amazing friendships
along the way. As a result, I have become PASSIONATE about Biz! So, when I had just a couple weeks to
decide whether or not to purchase Biz to Biz, I jumped in with both feet and haven’t looked back!
What exactly am I passionate about? Business. Growth. Community. Mentorship. Personal &
Professional Development. MEMBERS’ SUCCESS STORIES!! #BetterEveryDay

It’s my mission to make Biz to Biz the BEST & MOST COMPREHENSIVE organization for micro-small
businesses and sales professionals to use to help propel them in achieving their goals. And, by doing so,
to make a positive impact and INCREASE the overall success rate of all small businesses.

When I first took over ownership in January 2023, I knew I needed help, so the first big decision I made
was to rehire Tami Sparks. She has her own Biz story and is just as passionate about Biz as I am! She
brings with her a wealth of knowledge and experience and I’m honored to have her on the Biz A-team!!

Working arm-in-arm with Tami, we’re striving to focus on our members, community, growth and
mentorship. We’re excited to officially announce our NEW LOOK that I believe aligns much better with
our brand! And, if you’re an active Biz to Biz member, make sure to check out the updated Member
Center and Biz Resource Center! We’ve been listening to your feedback and I believe you’ll love the
changes!!

If you are an entrepreneur, a micro-small business owner and/or a sales professional …

In Biz to Biz, you will find your people, your tribe. We understand you!

Excited to travel with you along your Biz to Biz journey to success!!

_~Jennifer_
